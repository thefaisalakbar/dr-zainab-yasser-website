Her Journey

Trained with care. Practicing with purpose.

Dr. Zainab Yasser graduated with her Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 2025 and now works as a Dental Intern at Thumbay Dental Hospital in Ajman, where she treats patients across restorative, periodontal, and preventive dentistry.

Her approach blends clinical precision with a calm, reassuring chairside manner — helping patients feel comfortable from consultation through treatment.

What She Treats

Comprehensive general dentistry

Fillings & Composite Restorations

Pediatric Dentistry & Oral Health Education

Emergency Dental Care

Preventive Care & Hygiene Coaching

Personalized guidance on brushing, flossing, and diet to protect long-term oral health.
